About EduBridge AI

Learn for Free. Grow with AI. Build Your Future.

EduBridge AI brings free learning resources, AI-powered help, practice tests, career preparation and opportunity listings into one place so that students can grow regardless of their financial background.

Our principles

  • Original lessons, and links to official or openly licensed resources.
  • Scholarship and free-course listings point to official sources and show when they were last verified. Always confirm details on the official site.
  • The AI assistant can make mistakes, so please double-check important answers.
  • Respectful community: posts can be reported and reviewed by moderators.
